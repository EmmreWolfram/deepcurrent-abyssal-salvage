## 📖 Overview

**DEEPCURRENT** is a twin-stick survival shooter set in the crushing depths of Sector 9 Trench. The salvage beacon died at 3,000 meters. Something down there ate it. Now you must pilot the DSV-07 "Mantis" through waves of abyssal hostiles, salvage glowing motes for points, and survive long enough to uncover what lurks in the dark.

Every wave dives deeper. The pressure builds. The swarm adapts.

**Can you survive the abyss?**

## 🎯 How to Play

### Objective
Survive wave after wave of abyssal creatures. Destroy enemies to earn points and collect glowing orbs. Grab salvage capsules for temporary power-ups. Stay alive as long as possible — the deeper you dive, the higher your score.

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard/Mouse | Touch |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| **Move** | `W` `A` `S` `D` or Arrow Keys | Left-side joystick |
| **Aim** | Mouse cursor | Drag right side of screen |
| **Fire** | Hold `LMB` or `Space` | Drag right side (auto-fire) or tap **FIRE** button |
| **Pause** | `P` or `Escape` | Tap pause button (❚❚) |
| **Mute** | `M` | Tap mute button (SND) |

### Enemy Intel

| Enemy | HP | Score | Behavior |
|-------|----|-------|----------|
| 🟠 **Drift Mine** | 1 | 40 | Proximity bloom. Do not touch. |
| 🟢 **Angler Drone** | 3 | 80 | Homing bite, follows the light. |
| 🟩 **Ink Spitter** | 4 | 120 | Keeps range, spits hot bolts. |
| 🟡 **Leviathan Shard** | 16 | 400 | Radial volleys. Bring the spread. |

### Power-Ups

| Capsule | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **+** | Repairs 35 hull integrity |
| **S** | 6 seconds of shield (invulnerability) |
| **R** | 7 seconds of rapid fire (2.2x rate) |
| **W** | 7 seconds of wide spread (triple bolts) |
